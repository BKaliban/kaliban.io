## 🤔 What is this?

It's my personal CV website. Yes, an entire domain for one guy's job history. No, I will not apologize.

Instead of a boring PDF that dies in someone's Downloads folder, it's a single-page site with floating marble heads, glitch art, and enough neon to make a synthwave album cover jealous. If a recruiter doesn't remember me, they'll at least remember _"that weird statue website."_ Marketing.

## ✨ Features (allegedly)

- 🗿 **Two Greek statues** staring into your soul — free of charge
- 🌫️ **A vaporwave aesthetic** that peaked in 2016 and I refuse to let go of
- 📇 **My actual work history** — 6+ years of turning "it's broken" into "it's fixed"
- ✉️ **A contact form** that really works (please be nice, it has a honeypot and it's watching)
- 🎧 A footer that gets philosophical after midnight
- 🚫 **Zero cookies asking for consent**, because I respect you (and I'm too lazy to add a banner)

## 🔒 "It's just a static site, why is there security?"

Because I'm a support engineer and I physically cannot help myself. This repo has:

- 🛡️ **CodeQL** code scanning — for the three lines of JavaScript that could theoretically go rogue
- 🔑 **Gitleaks** secret scanning — history is clean, no keys, no shame
- 📌 **SHA-pinned GitHub Actions** + least-privilege tokens — supply-chain paranoia, professionally applied
- 🤖 **Dependabot** — my tireless intern who keeps the dependencies fresh
- 🧭 A **Content-Security-Policy** doing its best on a host that won't send real headers

Overkill for a page with two statues on it? Absolutely. Would I do it again? Already did.
